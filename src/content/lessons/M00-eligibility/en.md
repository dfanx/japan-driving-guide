## Verify before departure

Do not treat every card labelled “International Driving License” as valid. Common visitor routes are a permit conforming to the 1949 Geneva Convention or an eligible foreign licence carried with an authorized Japanese translation.

## Carry the complete set

Confirm your original licence, IDP or translation, passport, and evidence of entry date. The shorter of the document-validity and lawful-stay limits controls. A rental company may also impose stricter rental requirements.

## Common mistake

Waiting until the rental counter to verify the documents, or assuming a phone-only or commercial card-style document is an IDP accepted in Japan.
