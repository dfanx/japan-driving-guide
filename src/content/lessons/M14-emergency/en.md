## After a collision

If safe, move out of traffic and switch off. Call 119 for injuries. Report the collision to police at 110 even when nobody is injured, then follow instructions and contact the rental company, insurer, and roadside assistance.

## Expressway breakdown

Move as far as safely possible and warn following traffic. After warning, do not remain inside the disabled vehicle; when safe, evacuate to a protected place such as behind the guardrail.

## Contacts

Police: 110. Ambulance and fire: 119. Common JAF road-service numbers are 0570-00-8139 and #8139. Also confirm the rental company's dedicated contact before driving.
