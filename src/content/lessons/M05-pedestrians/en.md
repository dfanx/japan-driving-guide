## Approach ready to stop

Unless a crossing is clearly empty, approach at a speed that lets you stop before it. Stop and yield when a pedestrian or cyclist is crossing or appears ready to cross.

## Do not pass a stopped vehicle

It may be yielding to someone you cannot see. Do not drive around it, and never block the crossing area.

## The safer question

Do not ask only whether the person has stepped onto the crossing. Ask whether you can stop smoothly if they start crossing now.
