## Reduce exposure, not just speed

For winter travel in Hokkaido, Tohoku, Hokuriku, Nagano, and similar regions, confirm winter tires and chain requirements. Brake, accelerate, and steer gradually, increase following distance, and slow when visibility falls.

## Current controls come first

Weather can lower on-road expressway limits or close roads. Follow live signs, chain controls, and closures rather than a planned itinerary or navigation estimate.

## Cancelling is a safety decision

A booked hotel or tight schedule is never a reason to drive through unsafe conditions. Delay, reroute, or stop driving when the risk is unacceptable.
