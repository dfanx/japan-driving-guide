## Expect bicycles

Cyclists may appear along road edges, beside your vehicle before a left turn, and from narrow intersections. Scan beyond motor traffic.

## The 2026 passing rule

When passing to the right of a bicycle without sufficient space, drive at a safe speed for the available gap. NPA guidance suggests about one meter or more where possible, and roughly 20–30 km/h when that space cannot be secured.

## Separate law from guidance

One meter and 20–30 km/h are official safety guidance, not fixed statutory thresholds for every situation. Leave more space when possible and slow markedly when space is tight.
