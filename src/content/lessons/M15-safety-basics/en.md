## Alcohol does not reset overnight

Do not drive while hungover or uncertain that alcohol has cleared. Do not provide a vehicle to a possible drink-driver, ask them to drive you, or knowingly ride with them.

## Do not handle a phone while moving

Do not make handheld calls or fixate on phone/navigation screens while the vehicle is moving. Pull into a lawful safe parking place before changing the route; a red light is not a safe messaging break.

## Belt every seat and prepare the right child restraint

Every occupant must wear a seat belt. A child under six generally requires an appropriate child restraint; older children who cannot use an adult belt correctly should also use a suitable restraint.

## Do not force the drive

Fatigue, medication side effects, illness, or poor attention should lead you to switch drivers, delay, or cancel. Legal eligibility does not mean you are fit to drive right now.
