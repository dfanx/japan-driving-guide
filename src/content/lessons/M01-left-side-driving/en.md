## High-risk moments

Japan drives on the left. Wrong-side errors are most likely when leaving parking, turning from a narrow street, entering at a T-junction, or driving on a quiet road with no traffic to follow.

## Check after every turn

Use the practical mental check, “the driver’s side should be nearer the center of the road.” It is not a legal rule; it is a fast way to detect that you entered the wrong side.

## Thirty seconds before moving

Locate the indicators, wipers, lights, hazards, selector, parking brake, fuel-door release, and ETC unit before driving so you do not search for them in motion.
