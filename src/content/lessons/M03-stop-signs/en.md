## STOP means stop

Bring the wheels to a complete stop before the stop line. Without a line, stop at the required position before entering the intersection. Moving very slowly is still not a complete stop.

## The law does not prescribe a three-second count

What matters is that the wheels actually stop and that you make an effective check. A short mental count can help prevent a rolling stop, but it is not the legal test—and waiting three seconds without looking is not safe observation.

## When visibility is blocked

Stop at the legal position first, then creep forward at very low speed to obtain a safe view. Do not roll beyond the line and count that slowdown as the stop.

## Move again

Check both directions, including cyclists and pedestrians, and proceed slowly only when it is safe.
