## Learn the signs visitors actually need

Prioritize stop, slow, no entry, one way, no parking or stopping, maximum speed, no U-turn, pedestrian crossing, railway crossing, and ETC-only signs.

## Current signs override navigation

Navigation data may be old. If it directs a turn but a current sign, restriction, or lane marking forbids the move, follow the on-road control.

## Do not guess symbols

If a sign is unfamiliar, do not infer its meaning from a similar home-country symbol and proceed. Choose a safe route that does not conflict with the visible control.
