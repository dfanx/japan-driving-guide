## Right turns cross opposing traffic

Because Japan drives on the left, a right turn crosses opposing traffic. Yield to oncoming straight and left-turning vehicles and to pedestrians crossing your path. A green light is not absolute priority.

## Check left before a left turn

Use mirrors, check the vehicle’s left side, and turn slowly around cyclists, scooters, and pedestrians. A near-side turn can still contain conflicts.

## Choose the turn lane early

Follow lane arrows and move into the correct lane early. Do not cross a yellow solid boundary at the last second to obey navigation. A guide strip organises traffic; it is not a queue-jumping lane. If you miss the lane, continue and reroute safely.

## Uncontrolled minor intersections

Look for stop or priority signs, road hierarchy, and blocked sight lines. Slow down when uncertain rather than relying on one oversimplified priority slogan.
