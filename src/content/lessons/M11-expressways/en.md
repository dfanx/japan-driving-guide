## Merge and use lanes correctly

Accelerate to a suitable merging speed before joining the mainline. Use the left lane for normal driving and the right lane for passing, return after overtaking, and never use the shoulder as a lane.

## In congestion, merge one vehicle at a time

When traffic queues at a merge, use the merge lane to its end and alternate with the mainline one vehicle at a time, like a zipper. Do not force an early merge far upstream or follow another merging vehicle through as a pair. NEXCO promotes this method, but it does not give you priority: signal, leave space, follow the markings and merge only when safe.

## ETC lanes

Enter an ETC-only lane only after confirming the rental vehicle has working ETC equipment and a usable card. Otherwise use a general or compatible shared lane.

## A missed exit

Never reverse, turn around, drive against traffic, or stop at the split. Continue to the next safe exit or facility and reroute. Refuel early on long trips.
