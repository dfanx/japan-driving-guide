# F031 Reference-Image Audit — 2026-08-12

## Purpose

The 13 supplied textbook photographs are discovery inputs, not production
assets or legal sources. The course may reuse a verified driving concept, but
must not copy the book's layout, arrows, car artwork or wording. Production
diagrams remain original deterministic SVGs backed by approved Rules and
official Sources.

## Speed-enforcement post audit

Accepted:

- Japan uses fixed, semi-fixed and portable speed-enforcement equipment.
- Enforcement can be planned around crash conditions, priority routes and
  time periods.
- The 2026-09-01 local-road statutory-limit change has a defined scope and
  explicit effective date.

Rejected from learner guidance:

- `+30 km/h` or `+40 km/h` as a supposedly safe allowance;
- following a speeding lead vehicle;
- assuming police stop only the first vehicle;
- camera apps, detector products, warning cones or enforcement flashes as a
  substitute for reading the speed limit;
- ticket-colour, criminal-record and immigration claims without case-specific
  primary legal analysis.

## Production impact

- D011 is redrawn as an original road-type comparison with explicit teaching
  labels and no imitation of a regulatory sign.
- D003, D006, D014, D015, D019, D020 and D023 remain the reviewed original
  diagram coverage for the verified photograph concepts.
- New law/guidance is traced to S26–S29 and remains distinct by classification.
