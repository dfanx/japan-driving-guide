# F035 visitor tips claim audit

Checked: 2026-08-14

The supplied text is treated as discovery input, not as authority. The learner
copy keeps useful recognition and operating steps while separating legal duty,
official operator guidance and practical advice.

| Topic | Decision | Course treatment | Source IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right turn on circular green | Adopt | Yield to opposing straight/left-turning traffic and crossing users; green is not priority. | S16, S29 |
| Green right arrow means every conflict is red | Narrow | The arrow permits the indicated turn and removes much of the opposing straight conflict, but the driver must still scan for people and uncleared traffic. | S10, S16, S29 |
| Fixed two-to-three-second red-light clearance window | Reject as a timing rule | A vehicle that lawfully entered on green may clear after the signal changes without obstructing newly released traffic. A vehicle still behind the stopping position on red may not enter. | S16, S29 |
| STOP requires exactly three seconds | Reject as law; retain as optional self-check | Complete wheel stop and effective observation are the requirements. A mental count may prevent rolling but is not the legal test. | S03, S05, S16, S29 |
| Flashing yellow proves the driver is on a major road | Reject | Flashing yellow means proceed with caution. Flashing red requires a complete stop. Neither wording creates absolute priority. | S10, S16, S29 |
| Every solid white line prohibits crossing; double white is absolute | Reject | Yellow same-direction lane boundaries control lane changing. White, centre, edge and roadside lines have different meanings; drivers must read the applicable marking/control. | S16, S28, S29 |
| Right turn across a line into a shop is always legal if no sign is posted | Narrow | A missing entrance notice does not itself prohibit the turn, but crossing/turn controls, opposing traffic, pedestrians, sidewalk stop and obstruction duties still apply. | S16, S29 |
| Ordinary-road +30 / expressway +40 is criminal-procedure boundary | Adopt carefully | At or above the thresholds the case is outside the blue-ticket simplified ranges. Do not state automatic arrest or immigration refusal, and never present the threshold as an allowance. | S16, S34 |
| Okinawa Expressway is 80 km/h | Narrow | Much of the mainline is 80 km/h, but 60 and 40 km/h controlled sections exist; the posted section controls. | S16, S35 |
| Kei cars have weak brakes; Okinawa limestone makes roads inherently slippery | Reject | Teach vehicle-neutral wet-road actions: slow early, extend distance and brake smoothly. No adequate authority evidence supports either blanket causal claim. | S13, S14 |
| ETC, General and Smart IC operation | Adopt with distinctions | Ordinary ETC: 20 km/h or less. General/shared lanes: follow payment and ticket instructions. Smart IC: ETC-only and complete stop. | S12, S36 |
| Kishaba has no non-ETC lane | Narrow and date-bound | It is a Smart IC with ETC-only, direction and operating-hour restrictions. Tell travellers to recheck the operator page on the trip date. | S36 |
| Three common car-park equipment flows | Adopt | Teach barrier, lock-plate and camera-recognition identification/payment steps. | S25, S38 |
| `最大料金` is simply a capped total | Correct | It can cover fixed hours, a time band or only the current calendar day. Read conditions and cross-midnight effects. | S37 |
| AI will calculate the parking fee accurately | Reject guarantee; retain assistive use | Translation/AI may help parse a photo, but OCR, time, holiday and recurrence assumptions must be checked against the sign and payment machine. | S37 |
| Rental gasoline is always Regular/red nozzle | Narrow | Confirm the rental specification first. Colour is only an aid; do not infer fuel from vehicle size. | S14 |
| Cash fuel payment always prints a QR receipt for change | Narrow | Some prepaid stations print a receipt whose barcode is scanned at a separate change machine. Equipment and settlement vary by station. | S14, S39 |

## Editorial result

- Adopted operating value: seven tip groups.
- Removed unsafe shortcuts: fixed seconds, absolute priority, line-colour
  folklore, enforcement tolerance, automatic legal/immigration outcomes, vehicle
  stereotypes and guaranteed AI accuracy.
- Production diagrams remain unchanged because the affected lessons already
  pair reviewed scenarios with deterministic diagrams. F035 changes instruction,
  Source/Rule traceability and verification only.
